Key principles

Discrete levels. Electrons occupy permitted energy states only, never inside the forbidden gap.

Higher energy, farther out. Outer valence electrons have the most potential energy. Once freed into the conduction band, they sit at the highest energy.

Conduction needs room. Current flows only when mobile charges sit in states next to empty, available levels.
